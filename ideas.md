## Selected approach: Editorial Signal

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design fused with premium product-design systems: Swiss typographic discipline, modern startup interface polish, and understated magazine art direction.

### Core Principles
1. Content carries the motion; the background stays quiet and intentional.
2. Founder credibility comes from specificity, restraint, and clear distinctions between what is building now and what is long-term vision.
3. Asymmetry creates energy: copy and visual evidence should sit in a deliberate tension rather than a generic centered stack.
4. Every interaction should feel precise, fast, and useful.

### Color Philosophy
Near-black charcoal establishes seriousness and gives the portfolio the tone of a founder’s working desk. Warm white softens the interface and creates an editorial page-like feel. Electric cyan is used sparingly as a signal for action, product energy, and the path from Nepal toward a wider map; it should never become a decorative glow.

### Layout Paradigm
A long-form editorial canvas with a sticky utility navigation, a split hero, offset section numbers, horizontal story bands, and wide case-study moments. On smaller screens the composition becomes a vertical reading path with preserved hierarchy rather than a shrunken desktop grid.

### Signature Elements
- Tiny cyan route marks and rules that imply direction without becoming a map or particle effect.
- Oversized section numerals paired with compact editorial labels.
- A recurring “building / vision” status language that distinguishes current work from future ambition.

### Interaction Philosophy
Hover states should reveal intent: underlines draw in, cards lift slightly, arrows travel a few pixels, and navigation highlights the current section. Focus states are visible and elegant. Nothing should move merely to look alive.

### Animation
Use line-by-line hero reveal, small upward fades for section content, and staggered capability rows. Animate only opacity and transform, keep transitions under 300ms, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid particles, floating dots, animated blobs, and decorative background motion.

### Typography System
Use Space Grotesk for display headlines and labels, paired with DM Sans for readable body copy. Headlines are compact, bold, and slightly tight. Body copy stays relaxed with a comfortable line-height. Metadata uses uppercase labels with tracking and a smaller size.

### Brand Essence
A founder’s portfolio for people who want to understand what Ganesh is building from Nepal, how he thinks about products, and where the work could go next. Personality: **focused, curious, practical**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are short and declarative. CTAs are direct and collaborative. Microcopy is candid about what is in development and what remains a long-term direction.

Example lines:
- “The goal is simple: build technology from Nepal that can travel beyond Nepal.”
- “Currently building the first useful version.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the generated geometric mark as a compact symbol: a forward-moving path folded into a Himalayan peak and abstract G. Pair it with a custom-feeling uppercase wordmark treatment for the header rather than relying on a default logo lockup.

### Signature Brand Color
Electric cyan: `#7FE7E3`. It appears as a controlled signal color for links, active states, and the route motif—not as a full-surface background.
